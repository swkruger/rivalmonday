rivalmonday · decision labeler

Open the CSV written by pnpm --filter @cs/worker decisions export --out <file>.

Label each decision with the correct answer — not what you think the model said. Model answers are hidden by default to avoid anchoring.
Keys: ←/→ previous/next · 0–9 pick a score level · F mark remaining true/false questions false · N next unlabelled.

Nothing leaves this machine. Your progress is kept in this browser until you save.
