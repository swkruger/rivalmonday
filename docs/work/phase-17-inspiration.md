# Phase 17 — marketing site inspiration (Mobbin "RivalMonday Site")

Mobbin "RivalMonday Site" (private, 25 sections, read 2026-10-28). **All 25 are Sequence** (B2B billing SaaS): one coherent system, not a mood board. No comments. Gap fills from search are marked **[not owner's]**.

## Mood (whole site)
Light, enterprise-calm. White sheet, faint vertical grid lines, soft blue/lilac blurred washes behind heroes. **Light serif display headings** over sans body; one indigo accent (buttons, eyebrows, checks); an eyebrow label above every heading. Product UI floats on soft shadows. No illustrations or stock photos (one founder photo).

## Per-section patterns
- **Announcement bar + nav:** gradient strip "Announcing Sequence 2.0 →"; logo, 5 centred items with dropdowns, "Sign in" right.
- **Hero:** two variants. (a) left serif headline, 2-line sub, dark pill "Book a demo" + ghost "Take a tour", screenshot with play button right, logo strip under the fold. (b) centred sub-page hero: eyebrow, headline, sub, then a **live-looking UI card** instead of a screenshot.
- **Logo strip:** 2 rows of greyscale wordmarks, G2 stars above; reused under testimonials.
- **Product showcase / features:**
  - Alternating split rows: eyebrow, heading, copy, "Explore →" vs **composited UI fragments** (floating table, toggle, calendar, bar-chart cards).
  - Hub diagram: document centre with "Extracting pricing…" chip, integration tiles wired left/right.
  - Wide data table on a faint grid with an AI prompt box docked under it.
  - Tabbed panel: left list of 3 benefits, right a UI card on a blue texture.
  - Suite grid: 10 icon + title + one-liner tiles, no cards.
- **How it works:** 4-step numbered stepper above one large screenshot.
- **Social proof:** story cards with **metric stacks** ("<4 weeks", "90%", "3x" + caption), "Migrated from Stripe" tag; quote bento (1 large + 4 small); single quote card over a logo row.
- **Pricing:** 3 cards (one priced, two "Bespoke / Book a demo"), audience line per tier, checklist + add-ons; grouped **feature matrix**; 5-item FAQ accordion.
- **CTA band:** centred line over blurred UI fragments, one pill.
- **About / blog:** founder story + photo; blog = featured post, 3 cards, category chips, list, pagination.
- **Footer:** 3 link columns + **CTA card** (Book a call, G2, product tour).

## Takeaways for Rival Monday
1. Copy Sequence's restraint: white sheet on #F6F9FC, faint grid lines, one soft blue wash in the hero (#47A8E7/#2A6BAC at low opacity). Only #F5A524 is loud, kept for the Friday pill and one highlight per screen.
2. Hero: left-aligned 2-line headline aimed at agencies ("Know what your clients' rivals did. Every Monday."), primary "Join the pilot" + ghost "See a sample brief", and a real brief/dashboard frame on the right.
3. Show the product as composited fragments drawn from real UI: a rival-change card with its evidence screenshot, a rankings map tile, a Friday answer with a citation chip. Build them in HTML, not PNGs.
4. Make "no evidence, no claim" the hub diagram: one brief in the centre with evidence sources (website, Google reviews, ads library, maps rankings, social) wired in, and a "Verifying…" chip in place of "Extracting pricing".
5. Put Friday in a feature row like Sequence's AI prompt: a real rivals table with the Ask box docked under it, plus a typed question and a cited answer.
6. Use a 4-step stepper for "How it works": Add client → We find rivals → We watch weekly → Monday brief + alerts.
7. Social proof during the pilot: no fake logos. Use metric-stack cards built from pilot data ("12 rival changes caught in week 1") and an "In pilot with N agencies" eyebrow. Leave room for the logo strip later.
8. Pricing: Sequence's 3-column card layout carrying our real model: Trial (1 client, free), Standard $229 incl. 3 seats, Wholesale/Partner "Talk to us". Add an audience line per tier, a short feature matrix and a 5-question FAQ (white-label, data sources, MCP, cancel).
9. Add a dedicated agency/partner page in the Retool style: offer panel + application form (agency name, website, client count, verticals). It doubles as the pilot waitlist.
10. Comparison page: a check/cross table in the Notion style on a grey #EEF2F6 panel (Rival Monday vs DIY spreadsheets vs generic SEO tools), followed by 3 reason cards. Name categories, not vendors.
11. Footer CTA card ("Book a 15-min walkthrough"), plus a light announcement bar for pilot news ("Pilot open for HVAC & dental agencies →").
12. Typography: stay on Inter (binding). Get the editorial feel from large Inter 500 with tight tracking; a serif display face needs explicit owner sign-off.
