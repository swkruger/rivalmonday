Each phase has its own detailed plan (written when the previous phase is merged, so it builds on real code), ends in working, tested software, and is small enough to review as one branch.

## Done phases (summary)

Full detail lives in each plan and in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md).

- ✅ **Phase 1 — Foundations (2026-09-30).** Monorepo, RLS tenancy, tool registry with audit, AI layer with cost ledger, vertical packs, pg-boss worker, CI.
- ✅ **Phase 2a/2b — Evidence, web & vendor sources (2026-10-01).** Immutable evidence vault, polite Playwright collector, DataForSEO/Apify/ScrapeCreators collection; live-verified against Aire Serv.
- ✅ **Phase 3a–3c — Engine (2026-10-01/02).** Web and structured diffs → tagged, merged, scored events; moves; review themes and price tracking.
- ✅ **Phase 3d — Model ops & engine hardening (2026-10-03).** Jev shadow sampling + gold labels and accuracy report, Anthropic Message Batches, `decision_review` resolution, web-diff hardening; live-verified (96 % Jev/LLM agreement).
- ✅ **Phase 4a — Briefs, verifier & recommendations (2026-10-03).** Weekly brief pipeline with deterministic + Noul verification, AM review, recommendations and playbooks; migrations `0028`–`0030`.
- ✅ **Phase 4b — Alerts & delivery (2026-10-04).** Throttled alerts, client alert modes, Monday delivery, notification outbox (in-app/Postmark/Slack/Teams), signed deep links, branded PDFs, quarterly report; migrations `0031`–`0032`.
- ✅ **Phase 5a — App foundation & auth (2026-10-05, `3044e61`).** Better Auth, `@cs/tools` + `@cs/ui`, themed `apps/web` shell and delivery routes; migrations `0033`–`0035`.
- ✅ **Phase 5b/5c — Agency workflow, operations & client workspace (2026-10-06 → 10-09).** Portfolio, approvals, prospecting, usage, playbooks, workspace modules, data views and responsive shell; owner verification signed off 2026-10-11 (`e1088c0`).

**Phase dependencies:** 1 → 2 → 3 → 4; 5 can start after 1 (UI against seeded data) but its data modules land after 3; 6 needs 1 + 3 (tools read engine output); 7 last.
