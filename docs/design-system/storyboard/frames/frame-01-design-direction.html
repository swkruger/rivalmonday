Principles

Five rules every screen is checked against.

01

One white sheet, not card soup

Sections on white, divided by space and hairlines. Cards only for objects you pick.

02

Colour means something

Neutral frame. Colour marks an action, a selection, a status or a data series. Nothing decorative.

03

Friday is the only orange

The assistant pill and star ratings own #F5A524. Warnings use a darker amber, never the accent.

04

No evidence, no claim

Every insight carries a dated citation pill that opens the source.

05

Flat until it floats

Static surfaces have no shadow. Only menus, dialogs, toasts and bars that hover over content do.

We avoid
Rainbow status pills and decorative gradients
Cards inside cards, shadows on static boxes
Purple sparkles for AI; Friday is a name, not magic
