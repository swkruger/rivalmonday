15c-3a: agency management, mockups

This covers the platform's agency detail page at /platform/agencies/<id>: rename, people, hold and archive, plus the "On hold" and "Archived" filters on the agency list. Plans (create plan, change plan) come in 15c-3b. Layout follows design system v1: one white sheet, sections before cards. All copy is a draft for your sign-off.

Decided

  • Split: 3a = rename, people, hold, archive; 3b = plans
  • Archive refuses while billing is live: "cancel in Stripe first"
  • "Archived" filter on the list; only owners can unarchive
  • A hold freezes all billing work (daily job, Connect, activate/resume)

Decided (cont.)

  • Staff invites and resends count toward the agency's limits; the email names the agency
  • "On hold" chip + filter on the agency list
  • Starting or lifting a hold emails the agency admins; the email is generic, and the reason stays internal
  • Branding privacy/site URLs wait for 13a

Roles

  • Viewer: sees everything, no Manage menu
  • Staff: rename, people, hold/lift hold
  • Owner: also archive/unarchive (and plans in 3b)
  • Every change goes through a confirm dialog
