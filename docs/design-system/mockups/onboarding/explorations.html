Agency and client onboarding: mockups for discussion

Direction: onboarding sits outside the app (no sidebar), one idea per step, only required inputs, progress always visible. The left half holds the step and the right half shows a live preview of what is being built, or an illustration where there is nothing to preview yet. Not final copy or final fields.

Pages in this PDF

  • 1 · Agency: about you (3 required fields)
  • 2 · Agency: make it yours, with a live white-label preview
  • 3 · Client: find the business, with a map preview
  • 4 · Client: pick competitors, with a live "Where you stand" preview
  • 5 · Option: the same step as a split dialog over the app

Shared rules

  • Full screen, white form side, #F6F9FC preview side
  • Wordmark top left, "Save and exit" top right
  • Step label + segmented bar; Back · Skip for now · Continue
  • Required = red *, optional = muted "(optional)"
  • Saves on every step and reopens at the first open step
  • Below 1024px the preview panel is hidden

From Mobbin

  • Split with product preview: Lindy, HoneyBook, Maze
  • Split with illustration: Mixpanel, Asana, Remote
  • One question per screen: QuickBooks, Uxcel
  • Split dialog: Docusign, Squarespace
My guesses, for you to correct: agency onboarding goes from 4 steps to 3 (about you · make it yours · invite team), and plan/billing moves to a banner in the app; the long agency profile (legal name, tax ID, billing address) moves to Settings. Client setup keeps its 5 steps (find · services · competitors · invite owner · billing) with the same rules.
