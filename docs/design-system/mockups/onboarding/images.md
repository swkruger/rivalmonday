## Style brief (prepended to every prompt)
Calm, flat vector illustration with soft geometric shapes and gentle depth, generous empty space, on a very light blue-grey background (#F6F9FC). Palette limited to deep blue #2A6BAC, sky blue #47A8E7, warm amber #F5A524 as a small accent, dark navy #0B2540 for fine lines, and pale grey #EEF2F6. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere. No people's faces (no people at all). No real brands or products. Centred composition, square, subject fills about 70% of the frame, edges fade into the background so it sits on a light panel.
