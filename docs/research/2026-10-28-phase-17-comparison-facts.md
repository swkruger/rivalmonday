## 1. Fact sheets

### RivalMappd (rivalmappd.com)
- "Competitor intelligence reports and SEO content, delivered on a flat monthly subscription" for local service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, dental, med spa, law…) [rmp].
- Buyer: SMB direct. Also says "We offer white-label options for agencies managing multiple clients" and has an "Agency or reseller inquiry?" contact. What the white-label covers is not stated [rmp].
- Monitors: GBP, reviews (Google, Yelp), local search rankings, ads from the Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center ("every active Meta and Google ad") [rmp]. Website or promo change tracking: not stated.
- Cadence: monthly report; Dominance adds "Bi-weekly competitor intel updates"; first report within 5 business days [rmp].
- Evidence links or snapshots: not stated (sample report is a PDF) [rmp]. AI assistant, MCP or API: not stated. AI is used for blog content, "reviewed for local accuracy" [rmp].
- Pricing: Starter $299/mo (3 rivals, 2 blog posts), Growth $549/mo (5 rivals, 4 posts, up to 10 review responses), Dominance $799/mo. "No setup fees. No contracts." [rmp]
- Free trial: none listed [rmp]. Capterra lists the starter at $299/mo, free trial not available, US only, 0 reviews [cap-rmp].

## 3. Recommended comparison set

1. **RivalMappd.** The closest match: same US verticals, and it covers ads, GBP, reviews and rankings. *Rival Monday:* a weekly brief with website snapshots and evidence links, as self-serve white-label SaaS, from $229/mo for the agency. *RivalMappd is better at:* done-for-you work (blog posts, review responses, ad creative analysis, human review) and Google Ads coverage.
2. **BrightLocal.** The default local-SEO toolkit for agencies. *Rival Monday:* watches rivals' moves (sites, ads, reviews) and explains them weekly with evidence. *BrightLocal is better at:* citations, rank tracking, reputation tools, a mature API, and a low price per location ($31–$65/mo).
3. **Local Falcon.** The geo-grid specialist. *Rival Monday:* rankings are one input to a cross-channel brief. *Local Falcon is better at:* geo-grid depth, AI-platform visibility, an agent that acts on GBP and reviews, API/MCP/ChatGPT integrations, and credit pricing from $24.99.
4. **Birdeye (Competitors AI).** For agencies that already resell Birdeye. *Rival Monday:* adds website, ads and rankings, with evidence-linked claims and public pricing. *Birdeye is better at:* the full reputation/CX suite, an established reseller programme and multi-location scale.
5. **Visualping.** The DIY route. *Rival Monday:* local context (reviews, GBP, ads, rankings), plain-English weekly briefs, white-label. *Visualping is better at:* monitoring any page, checks every few minutes, screenshots, API and MCP on every plan, and a free tier.

Alternate: Grid My Business for budget agencies ($0–$89/mo, MCP).

## 4. Legal and tone notes
- **Never say "X doesn't have Y".** Say "As of 28 Oct 2026, X's public pages don't list Y", and link the page. ? cells in the matrix must not be published as ✗.
- **Date every page:** "Information as of <date>, from public vendor pages", with source links. Re-check quarterly; offer a corrections contact.
- **Prices:** quote exactly, with the billing basis (monthly or annual, per location, excl. VAT). Avoid "cheaper than X" unless the units match (Rival Monday is per agency; BrightLocal is per location; RivalMappd is per business).
- **Avoid superlatives** such as "only", "first", "nobody else". The study's claim that a US ad history is something "nobody else offers" is unsafe: RivalMappd monitors every active Meta and Google ad.
- **Trademarks:** rival names in plain text only (nominative fair use: no logos, lookalike styling or implied affiliation), plus a footer: "X is a trademark of its owner; Rival Monday is not affiliated."
- **Comparative advertising (Lanham Act § 43(a), FTC):** every claim must be truthful, substantiated and fair; keep a dated archive of the source pages (screenshot or PDF), in line with "no evidence, no claim".
- **Show where the rival is stronger** (section 3). It builds credibility.
