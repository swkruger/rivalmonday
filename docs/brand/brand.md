# Rival Monday — Brand Decisions

*Decided 2026-09-30 in the Phase 0 branding session.*

## Names
- **Product:** Rival Monday (wordmark: one word, lowercase — `rivalmonday`).
- **Default AI assistant:** Friday — the aide who prepares the brief at the end of the week; the product delivers it Monday. Agencies can rename the assistant.
- **Crawler User-Agent:** `RivalMondayBot/1.0 (+https://rivalmonday.app/bot; bot@rivalmonday.app)`.
- **Domains:** rivalmonday.app is the production domain (Namecheap). rivalmonday.ai is a possible future registration; confirm at the registrar.
- **Pending:** trademark attorney knockout search (main watch-out: monday.com marks in software classes).

## Wordmark
- `rival` in secondary **#2A6BAC** (white on dark/blue backgrounds) + `monday` in accent **#F5A524**.
- Inter ExtraBold (800), letter-spacing ≈ −1.5px at display sizes.
- "Friday" appears as a pill badge: background **#F5A524**, text **#3B2300**, weight 700.

## Typography
- **Inter** for everything: 400 body, 500 labels/UI, 600 headings and figures; 800 only in the wordmark (design system v1).

## Visual style
- **Superseded by design system v1 (2026-10-23, binding): see `docs/design-system/`.** #F6F9FC frame (top bar + sidebar) with one white content sheet; sections separated by space and hairlines, not cards; no shadows on static surfaces; 6px controls / 8px containers.
- Reference inspiration: Paige by Merchynt (card KPIs with change badges, highlighted CTA panel, per-tier automation modes, white-label settings).
