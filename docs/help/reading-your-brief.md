# Reading your weekly brief

Each week {{product}} sends a competitor brief for your business. It is also available in your dashboard, and you can download it as a PDF from the email.

## The summary

The brief opens with a short summary. In a week with real changes it is headed **This week**. If nothing important happened, the heading is **A quiet week**, and the brief says so instead of padding it out.

## The numbered items

Each item is a card with a headline about one competitor. Every item has the same parts:

- **What changed**: what was seen, such as a new price, offer, page or review pattern.
- **Why it matters**: what the change could mean for your business. This part appears when there is a clear reason.
- **What to do**: a suggested next step. This also appears only when there is one.

A line at the bottom of the card names the competitor and gives a low, medium or high rating for the effort of acting and the likely impact.

Every item rests on evidence. Use the **See the evidence** link in the email, or the evidence in the dashboard, to check the original source.

## Trends

Some briefs include a small trend table comparing businesses over a recent window of days. Use it for context rather than as a verdict.

## Next steps

Use **Open in your dashboard** in the email to see the full brief and its evidence. To go deeper, ask {{assistant}} a question about any item, and save a useful answer as a recommendation.
