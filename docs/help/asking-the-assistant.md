# Asking {{assistant}} about a client's competitors

{{assistant}} answers questions about a client's competitors and market, using the evidence {{product}} has collected from public information.

## Where to ask

- On a client's Overview page, the **Ask {{assistant}}** card lists suggested questions. Select one to open it in the assistant, or choose **Open** to start with your own question.
- You can also go straight to the client's Ask page. Past conversations are listed beside the chat so you can return to them or delete them.

Ask is available when it is switched on for your plan and role.

## Evidence in answers

Answers cite the evidence they rely on. Look for the numbered evidence chips in the answer, and select one to see the original snapshot or text.

## Saving an answer as a recommendation

Under an answer you can choose **Save as recommendation**. A dialog opens with a suggested title and rationale that you can edit before saving. The evidence the answer cited is attached to the recommendation.

## Confirm before anything changes

The assistant does not change anything on its own. If it proposes a change, such as saving a recommendation, a card shows what it will do, with **Confirm** and **Cancel** buttons. Nothing is saved until you confirm. After you decide, the conversation shows a short note such as "Saved." or "Cancelled."

## Tips

- Name the competitor or the topic you care about.
- Ask follow-up questions in the same conversation to build on an answer.
- Treat the cited evidence as the source of truth, and open it when a detail matters.
