## What this page tells you

People who joined the pilot waitlist from the public website. The page has three tabs, each with the number of new entries in brackets:

- **Agencies**: agencies that want to be invited. Each row shows the agency, the contact, the email address, how many clients it manages and its business type.
- **Businesses**: local businesses that want to be passed to an agency, with their business type and place.
- **Sample requests**: agencies that asked for a sample brief, with the business they named and how far the sample has got. A line above the table shows how many sample briefs you have used this month against the monthly limit, and what one sample is expected to cost: discovery up to $3.00 plus the average spend of the last starting points.

Entries are newest first, 25 at a time, with Previous and Next links. Use the search box to find an organisation, a contact, an email address or a sample business.

An entry marked **Not confirmed** has not yet opened the confirmation link in its email. It is shown so you can see demand, but it is not counted as new and cannot be invited, assigned or given a sample.

The number next to **Waitlist** in the menu counts confirmed new entries plus confirmed sample requests. An agency that also asked for a sample is counted twice: once as a new agency and once as a sample request.

## How to use it

1. **Pick a tab** and read the new entries. The status chip tells you where each one stands: New, Invited, Assigned or Declined. A sample shows Requested, In progress, Sent, Declined or Stopped.
2. **Search** when you are looking for one person or business.
3. **Follow a sample.** Once a sample is in progress, the line under its status shows the next step: Set up the client, Brief being built, Brief ready to review, Ready to send, then Sent.

The Actions column holds the buttons for the entry (invite, assign, approve or send a sample). Only owners and staff see them; viewers read only.

## Common questions

### Why can't I invite this agency?

Its email address is not confirmed yet, or it has already been invited. Only confirmed, new entries can be invited.

### Why is a sample request not counted?

Samples count only once the agency has confirmed its email address, and they use one of the month's sample briefs when you approve them, not when they arrive.

### Where does a sample go after I approve it?

The business is created in the Samples agency and its setup opens. Only the operator who approved the sample becomes a member of the Samples agency, so that operator finishes its setup; other operators approve their own samples. Run setup as usual, approve the starting point in Approvals, then send the sample from here.

### What should I do on the competitors step of a sample's setup?

Pick the rivals yourself. Do not use "Ask the owner to pick": a sample business has no owner, so the starting point would wait for the 14-day cap before it was built.

### What does Send again do?

**Send again** issues a fresh link and cancels the older sample links, so only the newest one opens.

### How long does a sample link work?

A sample link ends 30 days after it was sent. Send again if the agency needs more time.
