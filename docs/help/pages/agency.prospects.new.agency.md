## What this page tells you

Adding a business you are pitching uses the same guided flow as adding a client, with six screens instead of seven: **Client details**, **Find the business**, **What they do and where**, **Pick who to watch**, **Invite the owner** and **All set**. A prospect has no billing screen.

A prospect has no weekly monitoring, alerts or briefs: you pick its competitors and run a one-off snapshot, then convert it to a client when it signs.

## How to use it

1. **Enter the details.** We need the business name and a contact: their name, phone and email. These are suggested later as the person to invite.
2. **Find the business on Google.** Enter the name and a City or ZIP, choose **Search**, pick the match and check the business type. It sets which services you can pick and what the competitor search looks for.
3. **Continue.** This creates the prospect. Add keywords and a search radius on the next screen so the competitor search can run.
4. **Pick the competitors,** or choose **Ask the owner to pick** and invite them.
5. **Close** on the last screen takes you back to your Portfolio. Open the prospect from Prospects to run a snapshot.

If the business is not listed, choose **Can't find it? Continue without a listing**.

Only agency admins, and account managers who cover all clients, can add a prospect.

## Common questions

### What is the difference between a prospect and a client?

A client is monitored every week and gets alerts and briefs. A prospect is not monitored. Converting it makes it a client.

### Why do I need keywords and a search area?

They tell the competitor search where to look and what to look for. A snapshot needs at least one keyword, a search area and at least one competitor.
