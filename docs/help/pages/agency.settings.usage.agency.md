## What this page tells you

This month's credits used by each client against its monthly credit limit, with the competitors each client tracks and the Ask questions its people have asked. The month runs on UTC. Agency admins and account managers can open it. The page is read-only: the platform team sets each client's limit.

- The three tiles count the clients that are **Near limit**, **Limit reached** and **Paused**.
- **Credits** is the AI and data work tied to the client so far this month, as credits used of its limit.
- **Status** is that use as a percentage of the limit. From 80% it reads *Near limit*, at 100% *Limit reached*, and well past the limit *Paused*.
- **Competitors** is how many competitors the client tracks now, out of the most it may track. *Over limit* shows when it tracks more than its limit.
- **Questions** is the number of Ask questions the client's people have asked this month, out of the client's monthly quota. *At quota* shows when they have used it all.

Prospects are listed too, with a Prospect badge.

## How to use it

1. **Scan the tiles and the Status column** for clients that are near their limit or past it.
2. **Read what each state means.** *Limit reached* changes nothing: monitoring, briefs and Ask carry on as normal. *Paused* means AI and monitoring work for that client has stopped until the 1st of next month. Past briefs and evidence stay available.
3. **Contact support** if a paused client needs a higher limit.
4. **Read the note under the table.** Shared monitoring of a competitor (Google profile, reviews, ads, job posts) is split equally between the clients that track it. The intelligence engine's own shared work is not split across clients, so it is not in these figures. If you cover all clients, credits that are not tied to a client are shown there too, with the month's total.

## Common questions

### What happens when a client reaches its limit?

Nothing changes. The client shows as *Limit reached* here and on the Portfolio, and monitoring, briefs and Ask carry on as normal.

### What happens when a client is paused?

AI and monitoring work for that client stops until the 1st of next month, when the count starts again. Past briefs and evidence stay available. Admins see a notice at the top of every page while any client is paused, and the client's people are told their agency has been informed.

### Whose questions count toward the quota?

Only questions from the client's owners and viewers. Questions your own team asks use none and are never stopped. At quota, the client's people cannot ask more until next month.

### Why are the credits lower than I expected?

Work tied to a client is included, plus an equal share of each competitor's shared monitoring for every client that tracks it. The intelligence engine's own shared work is not split across clients.

### Why does a client show Over limit under Competitors?

It tracks more competitors than its current limit, for example after the limit was lowered. Remove competitors from the client's Competitors page.
