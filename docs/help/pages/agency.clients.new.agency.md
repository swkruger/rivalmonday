## What this page tells you

Adding a client is a short guided flow of seven screens, with a live preview on the right. This page holds the first two: **Client details** and **Find the business**. The client is created when you finish the second screen, and the rest happens in its setup.

The screens are **Client details**, **Find the business**, **What they do and where**, **Pick who to watch**, **Invite the owner**, **Set up billing** and **All set**. Every screen after the second can be left with **Save and exit**; the setup reopens where you stopped.

## How to use it

1. **Enter the client details.** We need the business name and the owner contact: their name, phone and email. The address and logo are optional. The preview shows the top of the brief they will receive.
2. **Find the business on Google.** Enter the business name and a City or ZIP, then choose **Search**. Pick the right listing from the results. A note appears if a result looks like a business you already have.
3. **Check the business type.** It is suggested from the listing and sets the topics, plays and alert levels the client starts with, and the services you can pick. If none fits, create a new business type; it is reviewed later and may be merged into an existing one.
4. **Continue.** This creates the client and opens the next screen, where you set keywords and the search radius.
5. **If the business is not listed,** choose **Can't find it? Continue without a listing**. We place the business from the address you entered, or ask for a City or ZIP on the next screen.

Only agency admins, and account managers who cover all clients, can add a client. The billing screen is shown to agency admins only. If your plan is at its client limit or your billing needs attention, the page tells you, and an agency admin can upgrade.

## Common questions

### How many competitors will it monitor?

You choose them on the **Pick who to watch** screen. Each client has a competitor limit, which you can see on its Competitors page.

### Can the owner choose the competitors?

Yes. On the **Pick who to watch** screen choose **Ask the owner to pick**, then invite the owner. They choose on their Overview.

### Is the client monitored right away?

Monitoring runs for active clients. The last screen tells you when the starting-point brief is due.

### Why do I need the owner's phone and email?

They are suggested as the person to invite, and kept on the client's record. You can finish the setup without them, but the invite screen is easier with them filled in.

### Should this business be a prospect instead?

If you are still pitching, add it under Prospects. A prospect gets no weekly monitoring until you convert it.
