## What this page tells you

This month's credits for {{client}}'s monitoring and AI work, compared with the client's monthly limit, plus the Ask questions its people have asked. The month runs on UTC. Agency admins and account managers can open it, and so can the client's owner, who sees the credits but not the questions.

- **Credits this month** shows credits used of the limit, a status label and a bar. The label reads *Near limit* from 80%, *Limit reached* at 100% and *Paused* well past the limit.
- **Ask questions this month** shows the questions {{client}}'s people have asked, out of the client's monthly quota.

## How to use it

1. **Check the label** when {{client}} is busy. *Limit reached* changes nothing; monitoring, briefs and Ask carry on as normal.
2. **Watch for Paused.** AI and monitoring work for {{client}} has stopped until the 1st of next month. Past briefs and evidence stay available. Contact support if the limit needs to be raised.
3. **See every client at once** on Agency settings, Usage & limits.

## Common questions

### What does Limit reached mean?

The client has used all of this month's credits. Nothing is switched off.

### When does the count start again?

On the 1st of each month, UTC.

### Why does the client's owner not see the questions?

The owner sees the credits only. The Ask question count is for your team.
