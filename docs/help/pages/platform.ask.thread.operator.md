## What this page tells you

One assistant conversation, read-only and as plain text. The subtitle names the client and its agency. Opening this page was recorded in the agency's audit log. **Deleted by user**: the person removed it from their list; it stays here for review.

Each message shows who spoke (the client, the assistant or a tool step), a status chip and the time: **Answered**, **Limit reached** (the question allowance was used up), **Couldn't answer**, **Something went wrong** or **Stopped**. Under a message you may see:

- **Citations**: what the reply relied on. A `kb:` citation links to its knowledge article.
- **Technical details**: closed until you open them. They hold the raw detail of an action message and the assistant's own log of how it answered.

## How to use it

1. **Read the thread top to bottom.**
2. **Follow citations** to the articles the answer used; open the article in Knowledge to check it says what the answer says.
3. **Open Technical details** only when you need to see how an answer was built.

## Common questions

### Can I reply or fix an answer?

No. This page only reads. Fix the knowledge article instead if the source was wrong.

### Why does the page say "not found"?

The conversation does not exist, or the client has not allowed review.
