## What this page tells you

What a visitor sees when they open your intake page: your logo or name, your colours, the search for their business and the contact form. Only agency admins can open this page.

- **The dashed frame** holds the page as visitors see it. Its top bar says *Preview — this is what visitors see*.
- **Link is off** appears in that bar when your link is switched off. Visitors cannot open the page yet; the preview still works.
- **Disabled in preview** sits beside the buttons. Nothing can be searched or sent from here, and nothing you type is saved.

## How to use it

1. **Check the look.** Your colours and logo come from Branding. Change them there and open the preview again.
2. **Copy the address.** The preview is not the live page. To share it, go back to *Intake link* and use *Copy link*.
3. **Turn the link on.** On *Intake link*, switch on *Accepting new leads*. If you have no link yet, choose a name and select *Create link*.

## Common questions

### Why can't I press the buttons?

The preview is for looking only. Visitors use the live page at your intake link.

### Why do I see "Link is off"?

Your link is switched off, so visitors who open the address see a "page not found" page. Switch it on under *Intake link*.
