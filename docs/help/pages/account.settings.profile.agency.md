## What this page tells you

Your own details and which notices reach you. This page is about you only: nothing here changes what your clients or teammates see or receive.

**Your details** are your name, your photo and the email address you sign in with. Your name and photo appear in the account menu at the top of every page. The email address cannot be changed here.

**Notifications** are the notices you receive, in the app and by email, plus a time zone and quiet hours for email. There is one card for each place you receive notices from, titled with its name: your agency, and any client you are also a contact for. Each card has a row per kind of notice with two switches:

- **In-app** puts the notice in your Inbox.
- **Email** sends it to the address on that card.

Your agency card lists **Client alerts (agency)**, **Brief ready for review**, **Brief failed**, **Brief overdue**, **Rank scan skipped**, **Quarterly report** and three setup notices that follow a client's setup: **Competitor suggestions ready**, **Client updated their setup** and **Setup waiting on you**. An account manager who covers only some clients gets these notices only for those clients.

## How to use it

1. **Change your name.** Edit it under Your details and select **Save**.
2. **Add or remove your photo.** Select **Upload photo** and choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP file up to 4 MB. It is cropped to a square. Select **Remove** to go back to your initials.
3. **Switch a notice on or off.** Every switch starts on. A switch saves the moment you change it, with no Save button, and applies to new notices only.
4. **Pick a time zone.** It sets the clock your quiet hours follow. Left on **Business time zone**, quiet hours follow US Central time (America/Chicago).
5. **Set quiet hours.** Fill in both a start and an end time, then select **Save**. A window can run overnight, such as 21:00 to 07:00. To turn quiet hours off, clear one of the times and save.

Emails that would arrive inside the quiet window are held and sent when it ends. In-app notices are not delayed.

## Common questions

### Why can I not change my email address?

It is the address you sign in with, so it is shown here but not editable.

### Where do I set up Slack or Teams messages?

Under Agency settings. Those go to a channel you connect, not to you personally, so they are not on this page.

### Why is there more than one notification card?

You receive notices from more than one place, for example the agency and a client you are also a contact for. Switches, time zone and quiet hours are saved separately on each card.

### Will quiet hours stop alerts to my clients?

No. Quiet hours only delay emails sent to you.

### Why do I get a notice by email and in the app?

Both switches are on for that row. Switch one off to get it once.
