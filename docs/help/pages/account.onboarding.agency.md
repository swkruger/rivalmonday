## What this page tells you

A short setup for a new agency, in four steps shown as a bar across the top. A preview on the right shows what you are building as you type:

1. **About your agency**: your agency name, a contact name, a phone number and an email address. The address is optional. The preview shows how you appear at the bottom of every client brief.
2. **Your team**: invite the people who will manage clients with you. The preview is the invitation email they receive.
3. **Make it yours**: your logo, brand colour and the name your clients see, with a preview of your client's dashboard.
4. **You're all set**: a summary of what you entered, then the choice to add your first client or go to the dashboard.

Only agency admins can open this page. Once the setup is finished or the Getting started card on your Portfolio has been dismissed, the address takes you to the Portfolio instead.

## How to use it

1. **Fill in your details and choose Continue.** We need the agency name, a contact name, a phone number and an email address. If you add an address, **Use this for billing too** keeps it as your billing address; turn it off and add a separate billing address later under Agency settings, Agency profile.
2. **Invite your team.** Enter an email and choose **Agency admin** or **Account manager** for each person, and use **Add another** for more. Each person gets an invitation by email. Admins manage billing and the team; account managers run clients.
3. **Put your brand on it.** Upload a logo, pick a colour and set the name your clients see. Text on your colour is kept readable.
4. **Check the summary.** **Edit** next to a row takes you back to that step. Choose **Add your first client** to start the client setup, or **Go to dashboard** to do it later.
5. **Skip what you are not ready for.** The team and brand steps have **Skip for now**. **Back** returns to the previous step, and **Save and exit** keeps what you have entered. Skipped steps stay on the Getting started card on your Portfolio, each linking to its Settings page, until you finish them or dismiss the card.

## Common questions

### Do I have to finish every step?

No. You can skip the team and brand steps and finish them later from the Getting started card. The agency details are needed first.

### Where do I change this later?

Under Agency settings: your profile, team and branding each have a page there.

### Why do I land on the Portfolio instead of this page?

The setup is finished or the Getting started card was dismissed.

### Why can an account manager not open this page?

Setup changes the agency itself, so it is limited to agency admins.

### Where is the plan step?

It is no longer part of this setup. Your plan and payment method are under Agency settings, Billing.
