## What this page tells you

{{product}} found businesses near you that customers may compare with {{client}}. Here you choose which ones to watch. Each one is compared with you every week in your brief.

The list shows what we found, grouped by how closely each business competes with you. The closest rivals start ticked. You can tick up to 5.

You reach this page from the card on your Overview that says your agency asked you to choose.

## How to use it

1. **Look through the list** and tick the businesses you want to watch, up to 5.
2. **Choose Confirm.** The button shows how many you ticked. You go back to your Overview.
3. **Leave without confirming** with **Back to Overview** if you would like more time. The card stays on your Overview until you choose.

## Common questions

### When does my first brief arrive?

Your first brief shows where {{client}} stands next to its competitors. It follows within 48 hours of your choice, once your business profile, reviews and first map scan are ready.

### What if I do not choose?

If you have not chosen after 14 days, {{product}} can start without you, using the data that is ready.

### Can I change my choice later?

Your agency can change the competitors for you. Competitors can also be added or removed from the Competitors page when your workspace allows it.

### A rival is missing. Can I add it?

Not from this page. Ask {{product}} to add it.
