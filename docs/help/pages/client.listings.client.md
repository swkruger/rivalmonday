## What this page tells you

Whether Google search finds {{client}} and its top rivals on the major online directories for your type of business, such as Facebook, Better Business Bureau and Yellow Pages. Customers often find a business through these listings, and a missing or inconsistent listing is an easy thing to fix.

- **Your listings** has one row per directory. **Listed** means Google search showed {{client}}'s listing on that directory, **Not found in Google search** means it did not, and **Not checked yet** means that directory has not been checked.
- For a listed row, the page shows whether the **name** and **phone** on the listing match {{client}}'s Google profile, and a link to the listing.
- **You vs top rivals** puts {{client}} next to up to 3 top rivals, directory by directory.
- **Also found on** lists other sites where Google search showed {{client}}'s listing. It only appears when there is something to show.

## How to use it

1. **Start with the gaps.** Each **Not found in Google search** row is a directory where customers searching Google are unlikely to find {{client}}.
2. **Add the missing listings.** Select **Add listing** on a row to open that directory's own sign-up page. Use exactly the same business name and phone as your Google profile.
3. **Fix what differs.** Where a row says **Name differs** or **Shows a different phone**, update the listing on the directory so it matches your Google profile.
4. **Compare with your rivals.** In **You vs top rivals**, a directory where your rivals are listed and you are not is a good place to start.

## Common questions

### How often is this checked?

Once a month. The date under **Your listings** shows when the last check finished. The first check runs once your competitors are set.

### What does "Not found in Google search" mean?

We search Google for the business by name and phone, then by name and city, and look for results from each directory. "Not found" means none of those results came from that directory. The business may still be listed there, but the listing does not show up when customers search Google.

### Why does it show a different phone?

The directory shows a different number than your Google profile. It may be a call-tracking number you set up on purpose, so check it before you change anything.

### Is the address checked?

Not yet. Each listed row says **Address: not checked**. Only the name and phone are compared for now.

### Why are my own listings missing?

The page needs your Google business linked in **Client settings**. Until then it shows a note instead of your listings, and the comparison shows your rivals only. If you can't change the settings, ask your agency to link it.
