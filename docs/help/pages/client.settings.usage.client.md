## What this page tells you

This month's credits for {{client}}'s monitoring, compared with the monthly limit. Credits measure the monitoring and AI work done for {{client}}. The month runs on UTC, and the count starts again on the 1st.

- **Credits this month** shows credits used of the limit, a status label and a bar.
- The label reads *Near limit* from 80% of the limit and *Limit reached* at 100%. When you reach the limit nothing changes: monitoring, briefs and Ask carry on as normal.
- *Paused* means monitoring and new briefs are paused for the rest of the month. Your past briefs and evidence are still here, and your agency has been told.

Only the client owner sees this page.

## How to use it

1. **Check the label** if you want to know how much of this month's allowance has been used.
2. **If it says Paused,** contact your agency. Monitoring starts again on the 1st of next month.

## Common questions

### What does Limit reached mean?

You have used all of this month's credits. Nothing is switched off.

### When does the count start again?

On the 1st of each month, UTC.

### Why do I not see this page?

Usage is shown to the client owner. Viewers do not see it.
