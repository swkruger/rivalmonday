This is a draft outline, not final legal text. It describes in plain words what the waitlist form collects and why.

## What we collect

When you join the waitlist we ask for:

- your name and email address
- your organisation name
- your website, or your city or ZIP code
- your business type and, for agencies, the number of clients you serve
- optionally, details of a sample business you would like to see
- where you came from: a `utm_source` value, the referrer host or the page path, stored with your entry
- a hashed form of your IP address, stored only to limit repeated submissions (rate limiting)

## Why we collect it

We use these details to contact you about the Rival Monday pilot and to decide who to invite. We do not sell them.

## Where it goes

The form sends your answers to our app at rivalmonday.app, where they are stored. We then email you a confirmation link that works for 7 days; entries that are not confirmed cannot be invited. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile as its bot check. It is a third-party widget that can see your IP address and browser signals.

## Who helps us run the site

We use service providers by role: hosting, email delivery, bot checking, error monitoring and cookieless page-view analytics. They process data only to provide those services.

## Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. Analytics are cookieless and do not follow you across sites.

## Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, email support@rivalmonday.app.
