This policy explains what personal information Rival Monday collects, how we use it and the choices you have. It covers this website, the pilot waitlist and the Rival Monday app.

# Who we are

Rival Monday is run by [Company legal name], [Company address] ("we", "us"). Rival Monday is sold to marketing agencies, who offer it to their own clients under their own brand.

If you use Rival Monday through an agency, that agency decides how your information is used for its service and we process it on the agency's behalf. Please send requests about your account to your agency first. You can also write to us and we will pass them on or help where we can.

# What we collect

## When you visit this website

- Page views, through cookieless analytics, with the query string and fragment removed from the address
- Technical details such as browser type, device type and IP address, which our hosting and security providers process to serve the site and protect it
- Error reports, if something on a page fails

## When you join the waitlist

- Your name and email address
- Your organization name
- Your website, or your city or ZIP code
- Your business type and, for agencies, the number of clients you serve
- Optionally, details of a sample business you would like to see
- Where you came from: a `utm_source` value, the referrer host or the page path
- A hashed form of your IP address, stored only to limit repeated submissions

## When you use the app

- Account details: your name, email address, role and the agency or client you belong to
- Sign-in and session details, including the time zone used to show you dates
- What you add: client and competitor details, agency branding, notes, settings and team invitations
- Questions you ask the assistant and the answers it gives
- Connections you approve for outside AI tools (MCP)
- A record of important actions on an account, kept for security and support

## Billing information

Payments are handled by our payment provider. We receive details such as your plan, payment status and invoices. We do not receive or store full card numbers.

## Public information about businesses

Rival Monday monitors local businesses using public information only: public web pages, Google Business Profile listings, Google reviews, public map results, public ad libraries and public job listings. We never log in to any site to collect it.

This information is about businesses, but it can include personal details, such as a name in a review or on a web page. Reviewer names are pseudonymized and contact details are removed before a review is stored. Contact details are also removed from text before it reaches an AI model.

# How we use information

We use personal information to:

- Provide the service, including briefs, dashboards, alerts and the assistant
- Create and secure accounts, and send sign-in links
- Send the emails you would expect, such as confirmation links, briefs, alerts, invitations and billing notices
- Run the pilot, including contacting you and deciding who to invite
- Take payments and keep financial records
- Keep the service reliable and secure, including preventing abuse and fixing errors
- Understand how the service is used so we can improve it
- Meet our legal obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

# AI processing

Briefs and assistant answers are produced with AI models from outside providers. We send them the information needed for the task, with contact details removed first, and we request zero data retention for those calls.

# Legal bases (EEA and UK)

If data protection law in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom applies, we rely on:

- **Contract:** to provide the service you or your agency signed up for
- **Legitimate interests:** to secure and improve the service, run the pilot and monitor public business information, balanced against your rights
- **Legal obligation:** to keep records the law requires
- **Consent:** where we ask for it, which you can withdraw at any time

# Who we share information with

We share information with service providers who help us run Rival Monday, by role: hosting and databases, file storage, AI models, email delivery, payments, bot checking, error monitoring, analytics and public data collection. They may use it only to provide those services. Our main providers are listed on the [integrations page](/integrations).

We also share information:

- With your agency, if you use Rival Monday through one, and with the people your agency or account gives access
- With outside AI tools you or your agency connect, limited to what you approve
- If the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users, the public or us
- With a buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganised, in which case this policy continues to apply until you are told otherwise

Each agency's data is kept separate from every other agency's.

# How long we keep information

- **Waitlist entries:** for as long as the pilot and any follow-up invitation need them, or until you ask us to delete them
- **Accounts:** for as long as the account is active
- **Archived agencies and clients:** removed from use straight away, and permanently purged no sooner than 30 days later
- **Evidence captured from public sources:** kept so that past claims can still be checked against what they were based on
- **Billing and legal records:** for as long as tax and accounting law requires

# Cookies and similar technologies

This website sets no cookies of its own. Its analytics are cookieless and do not follow you across sites. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile as its bot check, a third-party widget that can see your IP address and browser signals and may set its own cookies.

The app uses cookies needed to keep you signed in and to show dates in your time zone. It does not use advertising cookies.

# Security

We protect information with measures such as encryption in transit, access controls and database rules that separate each agency's data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

# International transfers

We mainly host in the United States, and our providers may process information in other countries. Where the law requires it, we use recognized safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

# Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Know what personal information we hold and get a copy
- Correct it or have it deleted
- Object to or restrict how we use it
- Move it to another provider
- Withdraw consent you gave
- Not be treated unfairly for using these rights

To use any of these, email [support@rivalmonday.app](mailto:support@rivalmonday.app). We may need to confirm your identity, and we may keep information the law requires us to keep. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, these rights include those under the CCPA. We do not sell or share personal information as that law defines those terms.

If your name or details appear in something we captured from a public source, such as a review or a web page, you can ask us to remove them from our records by writing to the same address.

# Children

Rival Monday is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

# Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell account holders by email or in the app.
